# OpenHDS Pro 2.0.118

## Form handling

- Draft forms now offer **Continue form**, **Cancel**, and **Discard draft**.
- Failed forms now offer **Open form**, **Cancel**, and **Delete failed form**.
- Destructive actions require a second confirmation and explain that deletion cannot be undone.
- Returning from an unfinished ODK form asks whether to discard it or deliberately keep it as a draft.

## Collected forms layout

- Finalised, draft, sent and failed cards now use consistent compact typography, icons, spacing and rounded corners.
- More form records fit on screen without losing their identifiers, status or form type.

Install this APK over the existing OpenHDS Pro app. Do not uninstall the app, because uninstalling removes locally stored application data.
